# AI Literacy Curriculum

**Open curriculum for teaching AI literacy to non-technical learners**

Designed by [Tessa Angelika Mmaitsi](https://tessa-angelika-portfolio.vercel.app/) — Learning Experience Designer and Cybersecurity Practitioner, Nairobi, Kenya.

---

## What this is

A practical, openly licensed curriculum for educators and facilitators who need to teach AI literacy to learners without a technical background. Built using evidence-based instructional design principles — scenario-based learning, spaced practice and application-focused assessment.

This curriculum is part of a broader project connecting learning design, cybersecurity education and responsible AI engagement.

---

## Who it is for

- Adult learners in professional contexts
- Community educators and NGO facilitators
- Workplace learning and development teams
- Secondary and tertiary educators introducing AI topics

---

## Curriculum structure

### Module 1 — What is AI and why does it matter?
Understanding AI systems without mystifying them. What AI can and cannot do. Where it already shows up in daily life.

### Module 2 — How AI systems make decisions
Pattern recognition, training data, outputs and errors. Why AI gets things wrong and what that means for the people using it.

### Module 3 — AI and your information
Data privacy, consent, surveillance and digital rights in an AI-enabled world.

### Module 4 — Working alongside AI tools
Practical skills for using AI tools responsibly — prompting, fact-checking, knowing when not to rely on AI output.

### Module 5 — AI, society and the future
Bias, fairness, automation and what it means to engage with AI critically as a citizen.

## Design principles

This curriculum is built on four commitments:

**Application over information** — every lesson connects to something learners can actually do or decide.

**Scenario-first** — concepts are introduced through realistic situations, not definitions.

**Critical engagement** — learners are not just taught to use AI tools; they are taught to question them.

**Adaptable by design** — each module can be delivered standalone, in sequence, or adapted for different contexts and time constraints.

## How to use this curriculum

Each module folder contains:
- A lesson plan with learning outcomes, timing and activity notes
- A facilitator guide with discussion prompts and common learner questions
- An assessment scenario with a rubric
